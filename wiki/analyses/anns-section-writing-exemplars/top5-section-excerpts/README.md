# Top 5 Section Excerpts

This folder contains local boundary-cropped PDF excerpts for the best five paper-section exemplars in each section-writing category. It is designed for private vault use and for quickly opening the relevant original pages without copying long raw text into markdown.

Background & Motivation is intentionally represented as one combined folder, not as separate preliminary and motivation folders. Use the combined folder when the target chapter needs minimum necessary context plus a bottleneck-driven motivation.

Cropping rule: start at the physical PDF page where the target section appears, and stop at the minimal physical page boundary that preserves the target section tail. If the next heading shares the same physical page, that page is kept; if the next section starts on a fresh page, it is not included. Page ranges are physical PDF pages, not necessarily paper-stamped page numbers.

Boundary audit status: renamed from `top4-section-excerpts` to `top5-section-excerpts` and regenerated on 2026-06-01. The old `03-background-preliminaries` and `04-motivation-characterization` excerpt folders were removed and replaced by the combined Top 5 Background & Motivation folder. Remaining adjacent content is due to two-column/page-level sharing or intentionally non-contiguous discussion/conclusion excerpts. See [Boundary Audit](boundary-audit.md).
